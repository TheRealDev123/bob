Welcome to Bank of Benny

The smart way to manage allowances, track investments, and approve real-world purchases.

📊 Virtual Banking

Keep a digital ledger of your balance and total spending securely in the cloud.

📸 Receipt Uploads

Use your device's camera to instantly snap and attach receipts to your purchase logs.

📈 Stock Tracking

Monitor the live Bank of Benny (BOB) stock price and watch your portfolio value grow.

✅ Admin Controls

Parents can review requested purchases, verify receipts, and deduct balances with one click.

How It Works

  1. Create an Account: Register your own username and secure password.
  2. Log a Purchase: Enter what you bought, how much it cost, and snap a picture of the receipt.
  3. Dad Reviews It: Benny logs into the Admin portal, views the receipt, and clicks OK.
  4. Automatic Deduction: Your virtual balance updates instantly!
